# Customer Interview - Sarah, Tech Startup Founder

**Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Participant:** Sarah Chen, Solo Founder at FlowTech  
**Persona:** solo_founder

## Background

Sarah is a solo founder building an MVP for workflow automation. She's been working independently for 8 months after leaving her corporate job. She embodies the Solo Founder persona - working with minimal resources and needing to ship fast.

## Key Pain Points

"The biggest challenge is figuring out which features users actually want. I'm building everything myself, so I can't afford to waste time on the wrong things. I need validation fast - like, yesterday fast."

"Every feature I build has to count. I don't have a team to iterate quickly, so I need to be really sure before I commit development time to something."

## Goals

"I want to ship my MVP as quickly as possible with minimal overhead. Speed is everything when you're solo. I can't get bogged down in analysis paralysis or over-engineering."

"I need lean processes that help me validate ideas without slowing me down. The whole startup world talks about 'move fast and break things' but when you're solo, breaking things means weeks of fixing."

## Product Feedback

"I wish there were tools that could help me validate features before I build them. Something that doesn't require a huge setup or learning curve. Just simple, fast validation."

"Most tools are built for teams with dedicated PMs and researchers. When you're solo, you need something that works with how you actually operate - scrappy and fast."

## Technology Approach

"I'm all about minimal overhead. If a tool requires extensive setup or ongoing maintenance, I won't use it. I need things that just work and don't slow me down."

"The MVP needs to prove the core value proposition quickly. I can't afford to build features that might be nice-to-have. Every sprint has to move the needle."

## Decision Making

"I make decisions fast out of necessity. I don't have the luxury of extensive stakeholder alignment or committee decisions. It's just me, so I need data that helps me choose quickly."

## Validation Methods

"I talk to users whenever I can, but I don't have a formal research process. It's usually quick conversations or feedback from early beta users. I need to make sense of this feedback fast."
