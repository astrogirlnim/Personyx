# Test Interview - Success Toast Demo

**Interviewer:** Can you tell me about your biggest challenge with product development?

**Interviewee:** As a solo founder, I struggle with knowing if I'm building the right features. I don't have a team to validate ideas with, so I often second-guess myself. I spend weeks building something only to realize users don't actually want it.

**Interviewer:** How do you currently validate your product ideas?

**Interviewee:** I try to talk to potential users, but it's hard to get honest feedback. People are often too polite and don't want to hurt my feelings. I end up with false positives and waste time on features that seem validated but actually aren't.

**Interviewer:** What would an ideal solution look like?

**Interviewee:** I need a way to get real, unbiased feedback early in the process. Something that helps me understand what users actually need, not just what they say they need. I want evidence-based decision making, not just gut feelings.

---

_This interview demonstrates clear solo founder pain points around validation and evidence-based product decisions._
