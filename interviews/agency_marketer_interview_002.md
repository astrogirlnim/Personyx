# Interview Transcript: Agency Marketer - Marcus Rodriguez

**Date:** 2024-01-22  
**Duration:** 52 minutes  
**Interviewer:** Product Research Team  
**Participant:** Marcus Rodriguez, Senior Marketing Manager at Velocity Digital  
**Persona:** agency_marketer

---

## Background

Marcus manages 8 client accounts at a mid-size digital marketing agency. His clients range from e-commerce to SaaS, with monthly ad spends from $50K to $500K. He's responsible for funnel optimization, creative iteration, and ROI reporting to C-level stakeholders.

---

## Key Quotes & Pain Points

### Copy Iteration Speed Challenges

**Interviewer:** "What's your biggest bottleneck in campaign optimization?"

**Marcus:** _"Copy iteration, hands down. I'll have a hypothesis about messaging - maybe we should emphasize 'time savings' instead of 'cost savings' for this SaaS client. But by the time I brief the copywriter, get drafts back, review them, and launch tests... **3-4 weeks have passed**. The campaign could be bleeding money the whole time."_

**Marcus:** _"I need to move at the speed of data, not at the speed of bureaucracy. **If I could get persona-aligned copy variations in hours instead of weeks**, I could test 10x more hypotheses per quarter."_

### ROI Reporting & Client Trust

**Interviewer:** "How do you currently report performance to clients?"

**Marcus:** _"Monthly decks, mostly. Revenue, ROAS, cost per acquisition. But clients are getting smarter - they want to know WHY something worked, not just THAT it worked. **'We increased conversions by 23%' isn't enough anymore. They want to understand the persona insights behind the wins.**"_

**Marcus:** _"Last month, our e-commerce client's CEO asked me: 'Which customer segments are driving the conversion lift?' I had campaign data, but no persona-level evidence. **I felt like a fraud charging premium rates without deeper insights.**"_

### Multi-Client Evidence Management

**Interviewer:** "How do you manage insights across different clients?"

**Marcus:** _"Honestly? Badly. I have folders full of customer research - surveys, interviews, focus groups - but it's impossible to quickly find the right insights when I need them. **Client A's persona research doesn't translate to Client B, but there's probably overlap I'm missing.**"_

**Marcus:** _"I'll be writing ads for a fitness app and thinking, 'Didn't we learn something about motivation triggers from that productivity client?' But finding that insight means digging through months of files. **By the time I find it, the opportunity has passed.**"_

### Funnel Optimization Workflow

**Interviewer:** "Walk me through how you optimize a conversion funnel."

**Marcus:** _"I start with analytics - where are people dropping off? Then I make hypotheses about why. But here's the problem: **my hypotheses are based on gut feeling, not user evidence.** Maybe the checkout page has too many fields, or maybe the value prop isn't clear. I'm guessing."_

**Marcus:** _"What I need is a way to cross-reference funnel drop-offs with actual user feedback. **If 40% drop off at checkout AND I have persona evidence that this segment values 'quick purchasing,' then I know to test a simplified flow.** Right now those connections happen by accident."_

### Competitive Pressure & Speed

**Marcus:** _"Agencies live and die by results. If I can't prove ROI improvement quarter over quarter, clients leave. **The winning agencies are the ones who can iterate fastest** - more tests, smarter targeting, better creative."_

**Marcus:** _"I need tools that make me 10x faster at connecting user insights to campaign performance. **Not more dashboards to check, but intelligence baked into my existing workflow.** I live in Google Ads, Facebook Ads Manager, and Slack - that's where I need the insights."_

### Evidence-Driven Attribution

**Interviewer:** "How do you connect campaign performance to user personas?"

**Marcus:** _"That's the million-dollar question. I can see that our 'productivity professionals' segment has a 40% higher LTV, but I can't easily connect that to which ad copy or landing page resonates with them. **I need persona-level attribution that goes beyond just demographics.**"_

**Marcus:** _"If I could score my campaigns against persona evidence - like, 'This ad has 85% alignment with productivity professional pain points' - I could predict performance before spending budget. **That's the difference between a good agency and a great one.**"_

## Key Insights for Evidence Engine

1. **Speed is critical:** Copy iteration must happen in hours/days, not weeks
2. **ROI justification:** Clients demand persona-level insights behind performance metrics
3. **Cross-client learning:** Need to surface relevant insights across different client accounts
4. **Funnel intelligence:** Connect funnel analytics with persona evidence for optimization
5. **Workflow integration:** Must integrate with Google Ads, Facebook, Slack - not standalone tools
6. **Predictive scoring:** Score campaigns/creative against persona evidence before launch
