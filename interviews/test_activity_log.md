# Test Activity Log - Interview Transcript

**Date:** January 16, 2025  
**Interviewer:** Personyx Team  
**Interviewee:** Solo Founder

## Interview Content

**Interviewer:** Tell me about your biggest challenge when launching your product.

**Interviewee:** My biggest pain point is getting real user feedback early in the development process. I need a way to validate my ideas before I spend months building something nobody wants. I'm a solo founder so I don't have a big team to bounce ideas off.

**Interviewer:** How do you currently handle product validation?

**Interviewee:** Right now I mostly rely on surveys and beta testers, but it's hard to get honest feedback. People tend to be polite rather than brutally honest about what's wrong with my product. I need better tools for collecting and analyzing user insights.

**Interviewer:** What would an ideal solution look like?

**Interviewee:** I want something that can automatically analyze customer conversations and extract actionable insights. Something that tells me what features to prioritize based on real evidence from user interviews and support tickets.

## Key Insights

- Pain point: Getting honest user feedback
- Need: Automated insight extraction
- Goal: Evidence-based product decisions
- Persona: Solo Founder
- Challenge: Limited team for validation
