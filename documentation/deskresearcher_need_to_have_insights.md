
# DeskResearcher – Making Customer Insight Indispensable

### Executive Snapshot
DeskResearcher **prevents wasted sprints** by flagging feature ideas that lack real‑user evidence and surfacing persona‑specific objections *before* code is written.

---

## Why DeskResearcher Is Currently Viewed as “Nice‑to‑Have”
- Value is positioned as convenience (“chat with personas”) rather than **risk or revenue protection**.  
- Benefits are framed around productivity boosts instead of **hard cost avoidance**.  
- No explicit tie to C‑suite metrics (feature failure rate, churn, roadmap accuracy).  
- Lacks an urgent, recurring trigger that forces adoption.  
- Exists as a standalone desktop app → extra friction in the day‑to‑day workflow.

---

## Five Strategic Levers to Become “Need‑to‑Have”

| # | Lever | How to Execute | Hard Business Impact |
|---|-------|---------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Reposition as Risk / Rework Prevention** | Market as the *pre‑code safety gate* that blocks unfounded features. | Saves 1–3 wasted sprints per bad idea. |
| 2 | **Attach to Exec Metrics** | Auto‑generate **Evidence Scorecards** PMs present in roadmap reviews. | Higher roadmap confidence, fewer failed launches. |
| 3 | **Align with Compelling Events** | Onboarding flows for: feature‑flop retrospective, new‑PM ramp‑up, quarterly planning. | Turns latent pain into urgent action. |
| 4 | **Own Churn / Retention Signal** | Surface persona‑linked churn drivers and missing capabilities before renewal. | Directly protects ARR and expansion revenue. |
| 5 | **Embed in Flow of Work** | VSCode, Notion, Linear & Slack extensions → not another window to manage. | Zero‑friction adoption → daily active use. |
