# Interview Transcript - Product Manager with Spéciál Châractérs

**Date**: 2024-01-17  
**Participant**: José María García-López, Señor Product Manager at TëchCorp™  
**Interviewer**: Research Team (François & Müller)  
**Location**: São Paulo, Brazil 🇧🇷

## Background & Context

José María manages múltiple products with names like "Açaí Analytics®" and "Coração Cloud©". His team works with clients from 15+ countries including España, Deutschland, 中国, and 日本.

## Pain Points Discussion

**Interviewer**: What's your biggest challenge with requirements gathering?

**José María**: "¡Ay, Dios mío! Working with international clients is complex. When a client from München says 'wir brauchen bessere Funktionalität' (we need better functionality), but our Brazilian team thinks 'funcionalidade melhor' means something completely different. Language barriers create confusion in requirements."

**Interviewer**: How do you handle multilingual requirements?

**José María**: "We have documents in English, Português, Español, and Deutsch. Sometimes clients send requirements like: 'Need feature that works like WeChat (微信) but for business use.' Cultural context matters as much as technical specs."

## Tool Integration Challenges

**José María**: "Our workflow involves tools with Unicode issues. When I copy requirements from Jira to Slack, special characters get mangled: 
- São Paulo → S?o Paulo
- Müller & Associates → M?ller & Associates  
- 中文名称 → ???

This creates confusion and errors in our documentation."

## User Feedback Examples

**Client Quote (Germany)**: "Die Lösung müßte eine beßere Integration mit unseren bestehenden Systemen haben."

**Client Quote (Spain)**: "Necesitamos una función que permita análisis en tiempo real."

**Client Quote (Japan)**: "リアルタイム分析機能が必要です。"

**José María**: "See the problem? Same requirement, different languages. We need better ways to standardize and translate requirements while preserving cultural context."

## File Naming & Organization

**José María**: "Our clients send files named things like:
- 'Spécifications Téchniques v2.1 - FINAL (1).docx'
- 'Требования_к_системе_2024.pdf' (Russian)
- '产品需求文档_v3.xlsx' (Chinese)

Our current system breaks with these file names. ASCII-only systems are 2020's thinking!"

## Success Metrics

**José María**: "Success for us means handling requirements in any language, with any character set, from any culture. When our German client can submit requirements in Deutsch and our Brazilian team can understand them perfectly - that's success."

---

**Post-Interview Notes**:
- Clear need for international/multilingual requirements handling
- Unicode and special character support critical for global teams
- Cultural context as important as technical translation
- File handling must support international naming conventions
- Tool integration often breaks with non-ASCII characters
